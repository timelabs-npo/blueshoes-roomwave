## Tester invitation page copy

**Find your flow. Then beat it.**

Roomwave is a small kinetic surfing arcade demo. Your timing changes the flight arc, momentum and score. Start with Neon Dawn, try the longer aerial lines of Low Orbit, then learn the dive windows in Black Current.

Photo and LiDAR creation are optional experiments: the app interprets local shapes into restrained variations of a known course. Custom routes remain local in this beta. Shared authored-course links require Roomwave to be installed.

Please send feedback through TestFlight with the course, what you expected, what happened, and your device/OS. A screenshot or short clip is useful. Avoid including personal photographs or home scans in public reports.
