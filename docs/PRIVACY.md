# Roomwave privacy policy

Effective: 25 November 2026. Applies to the Roomwave arcade beta distributed under the existing bundle `org.timelabs.moleculefoundry` by Time Labs NPO.

## Playing and local records

The game works without an account. Preferences, personal bests and any locally retained input replay are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Roomwave does not send these records to a game server. Removing the app removes its local records, subject to your device backup settings.

Roomwave does not include advertising, advertising identifiers, tracking SDKs, third-party analytics or a social-login requirement. It uses elapsed time to update gameplay and to judge whether a capture is still fresh; it does not use this information to fingerprint devices.

## Optional photo and LiDAR creation

Photo access starts only when you choose a picture through Apple's system photo picker. Analysis runs on your device. Camera access starts only when you explicitly choose device capture and grant permission. LiDAR capture requires a compatible device and uses ARKit mesh data to derive a stylized course.

Raw photographs, EXIF metadata, camera frames, mesh samples and local capture identifiers are not uploaded by Roomwave or included in a share card or challenge link. Temporary raw capture data is discarded when processing ends or is cancelled. Derived course geometry is a creative game representation; it is not a measured reconstruction of your room.

## Sharing

Sharing requires an explicit action. A score card or challenge link contains the game result and course identity shown to you. Choosing a destination in the system share sheet sends that selected content through the app you chose, under that service's own privacy policy. Roomwave does not automatically post to X and does not collect X credentials, follower lists, likes or reposts. Do not include personal details in feedback or public issue reports unless you intend to disclose them.

## TestFlight and diagnostics

Apple operates TestFlight and may provide the developer with installation, usage, crash and feedback information according to Apple's TestFlight terms and your settings. A screenshot or message you deliberately send as TestFlight feedback may contain information visible on your screen. Such feedback is used to diagnose and improve this beta. This is separate from the app's local-only gameplay and capture processing.

## Questions and requests

Use **Send Beta Feedback** in TestFlight to contact the developer privately about this beta or request help concerning submitted feedback. General product issues can also be reported at [the Roomwave repository](https://github.com/timelabs-npo/blueshoes-roomwave/issues); GitHub issues are public. Do not put private photographs, home scans, credentials or contact details into a public issue.

This policy will be updated before adding a service that changes these data flows. The beta does not require that service to play the current authored courses.
